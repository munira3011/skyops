# Zenith Air — Baggage Allowance Policy

## Checked baggage allowance by cabin class

| Cabin Class | Pieces Allowed | Max Weight per Piece | Max Combined Weight |
|---|---|---|---|
| Economy (Saver fare) | 1 | 23 kg | 23 kg |
| Economy (Standard fare) | 2 | 23 kg | 46 kg |
| Premium Economy | 2 | 23 kg | 46 kg |
| Business | 3 | 32 kg | 96 kg |
| First | 3 | 32 kg | 96 kg |

Each checked bag must not exceed linear dimensions of 158 cm (length + width + height combined). Bags exceeding this must be shipped as cargo.

## Cabin baggage allowance

- Economy and Premium Economy: 1 cabin bag up to 7 kg, plus 1 personal item (laptop bag or handbag).
- Business and First: 2 cabin bags up to 12 kg combined, plus 1 personal item.
- Cabin bags must fit within 55 x 38 x 20 cm to be accepted at the gate.

## Excess baggage charges

Passengers exceeding their allowance are charged per additional kilogram, calculated per route distance band:

- Short-haul (under 3,500 km): 25 USD per additional kg
- Medium-haul (3,500–7,000 km): 35 USD per additional kg
- Long-haul (over 7,000 km): 50 USD per additional kg

Excess baggage fees are waived for one additional 23 kg piece for Platinum tier loyalty members, and for active-duty military personnel traveling on orders.

## Special and sporting equipment

- Sporting equipment (golf bags, ski equipment, surfboards up to 2.5 m) is accepted as part of the standard checked allowance at no extra charge, provided it is within the standard weight limit.
- Musical instruments too large for the cabin may be booked an extra seat ("instrument seat") at 50% of the applicable adult fare, or checked as fragile baggage with a signed waiver.

## Restricted and prohibited items

Lithium battery power banks and spare batteries must be carried in cabin baggage only, not checked baggage, and are limited to a maximum of two spare batteries per passenger, each under 100 Wh. Sharp objects, flammable liquids, and firearms follow standard international aviation security restrictions and must be declared at check-in where applicable.

## Infant and child allowance

Infants (under 2 years, not occupying a seat) are permitted 1 checked bag up to 10 kg and a fully collapsible stroller or car seat, checked free of charge in addition to the standard allowance.
