# Zenith Air — Check-in & Travel Documents Policy

## Check-in windows

- Online and mobile app check-in opens 48 hours before scheduled departure and closes 90 minutes before departure for international flights, 60 minutes for domestic sectors.
- Airport check-in counters open 3 hours before departure and close 60 minutes before departure for international flights.
- Passengers with checked baggage must complete counter or bag-drop check-in no later than 60 minutes before departure; online check-in alone does not satisfy this requirement if checked bags are involved.

## Boarding

Boarding gates close strictly 20 minutes before scheduled departure. Passengers not present at the gate by this time may be offloaded and rebooked at standard fare-change conditions, not disruption-rebooking conditions, since a missed gate closure due to late arrival is passenger-caused.

## Travel document requirements

- All passengers must hold a passport valid for at least 6 months beyond the return date for most international destinations served by Zenith Air.
- Visa requirements vary by nationality and destination; passengers are responsible for confirming their own visa eligibility before travel. Zenith Air check-in staff will deny boarding if required visa or transit documentation is not presented, and this denial does not qualify for disruption compensation.
- UAE residents transiting through DXB on a connecting Zenith Air itinerary do not require a UAE transit visa provided the connection is completed within 96 hours and the passenger remains airside.

## Unaccompanied minors

Children aged 5–11 traveling without an accompanying adult must be booked under the Unaccompanied Minor service at least 48 hours in advance. A fee of 100 USD each way applies. Children aged 12–17 may travel alone without this service but a parent or guardian may opt into it voluntarily at the same fee.

## Special assistance requests

Requests for wheelchair assistance, medical equipment carriage, or dietary accommodations should be submitted at least 48 hours before departure through the booking management portal or contact center to guarantee availability. Requests submitted within 48 hours are accommodated on a best-effort basis.
