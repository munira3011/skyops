# Zenith Air — Delay, Cancellation & Rebooking Policy

## Rebooking eligibility

Passengers on a flight that is delayed by more than 90 minutes, or cancelled, are automatically eligible for free rebooking onto the next available Zenith Air flight to the same destination, in the same cabin class originally booked, at no fare difference.

If no Zenith Air flight is available within 24 hours of the original scheduled departure, passengers may be rebooked onto a partner airline at Zenith Air's discretion, provided the partner route is confirmed available in the same or higher cabin class.

## Rebooking process by delay cause

- **Weather or air traffic control delays**: Rebooking offered free of charge; no additional compensation beyond rebooking and standard care provisions (meals, hotel if overnight) applies, as these are classified outside airline control.
- **Technical or crew scheduling delays**: Rebooking offered free of charge, and passengers delayed more than 3 hours become eligible for the compensation schedule below, since these causes are classified within airline control.

## Compensation schedule (delays within airline control)

| Delay Length | Compensation |
|---|---|
| 3–6 hours | Meal voucher + 150 USD travel credit |
| 6–12 hours | Meal voucher + hotel (if overnight) + 300 USD travel credit |
| Over 12 hours or cancellation | Meal voucher + hotel (if overnight) + full refund option or 500 USD travel credit, passenger's choice |

## Human approval requirement

Any rebooking that changes a passenger's cabin class, involves a partner airline, or applies a compensation credit above 300 USD must be reviewed and approved by a Zenith Air agent before being finalized — automated systems may propose these rebookings but cannot confirm them without agent sign-off.

## Missed connections

If a delay causes a passenger to miss a connecting Zenith Air flight, the passenger is automatically protected and rebooked onto the next available connection at no charge, with lounge access offered for wait times over 2 hours regardless of loyalty tier.

## Voluntary changes (not disruption-related)

Passenger-initiated date or route changes, unrelated to a delay or cancellation, are subject to standard fare rules: Saver fares incur a 100 USD change fee plus fare difference; Standard and Flexible fares waive the change fee but fare difference still applies.
