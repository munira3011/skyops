# Zenith Air — Zenith Miles Loyalty Program

## Tier structure and qualification

| Tier | Qualifying Miles (rolling 12 months) | Qualifying Flights (rolling 12 months) |
|---|---|---|
| Silver | 0 (default on enrollment) | 0 |
| Gold | 25,000 miles | or 20 flights |
| Platinum | 75,000 miles | or 50 flights |

Members qualify for a tier by meeting either the mileage threshold or the flight-count threshold, whichever is reached first. Tier status is evaluated on a rolling 12-month basis and re-assessed each membership anniversary.

## Benefits by tier

### Silver
- Standard mileage earning rate (1 mile per mile flown in Economy)
- Access to Zenith Miles online booking rewards catalogue
- Priority standby on the same day only

### Gold
- 1.5x mileage earning rate
- 1 complimentary checked bag upgrade (extra 10 kg) on any fare
- Access to Gold-tier airport lounges when flying Zenith Air or a partner airline
- Priority check-in and boarding (Group 2)
- Waived date-change fee on Standard fares (fare difference still applies)

### Platinum
- 2x mileage earning rate
- Unlimited complimentary checked bag upgrade to next weight bracket
- Access to all airport lounges including First Class lounges, regardless of cabin booked
- Priority check-in, security fast-track, and boarding (Group 1)
- Two complimentary cabin upgrades per year, subject to availability, requestable from 72 hours before departure
- Dedicated Platinum service line for rebooking and disruption support
- One guest may accompany a Platinum member into lounge access at no charge

## Mileage redemption

Miles can be redeemed for flight awards, cabin upgrades, or partner hotel/car rental bookings. Redemption flight awards require a minimum of 15,000 miles for a one-way Economy short-haul award, scaling up by cabin class and distance band. Miles expire 36 months after the date they are earned if the account has no qualifying activity in that window.

## Family pooling

Silver, Gold, and Platinum members may link up to 5 family member accounts to pool miles into a single redeemable balance, while each member retains their individually earned tier status and benefits.
